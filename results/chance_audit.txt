--- Study 2: consistency of numerator vs denominator ---

scheme                               num   den P(>=num)
(i) all 4 groups                      17   312   0.3934
(ii) disjoint NT+TR                   10   156   0.2551

Only the INCONSISTENT scheme (double-counts overlap) reaches p<.05.

so ~1.3x dependence inflation (see perm_null.m) makes p even LARGER.
